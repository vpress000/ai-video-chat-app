## 🧑‍💻 Slide 1: Team Introduction (10 sec)
- Name and brief background
- Current status: school, job hunt, interview count
- Keep it short—just context

---

## 🧠 Slide 2: Problem Statement (10 sec)
- Define the target customer clearly
- Explain the specific challenge you're solving
- Show the real-world impact
- Mention your research to validate the problem

---

## 💡 Slide 3: Solution Overview (20 sec)
- Describe your approach and methodology
- Highlight any independent research
- Note alternative solutions you considered
- Emphasize autonomy and proactive work

---

## 🧱 Slide 4: Architecture Diagram (10 sec)
- Include 3 simple system components
- Clean and uncluttered design
- Visualize core flow without too many logos or APIs

---

## 🎬 Slide 5: Live Demo (30–40 sec)
- Demonstrate working product
- Use a deployed/production link
- Focus on smooth user experience
- Highlight key outcomes or features

---

## 🔍 Slide 6: Code Walkthrough (30–40 sec)
- Showcase the 2 hardest problems you tackled
- Explain technical challenges and how you solved them
- Emphasize depth in thinking and troubleshooting

---

## 🚀 Slide 7: Future Improvements (10 sec)
- Discuss what you'd enhance with more time
- Show forward-thinking and long-term vision

---

## 💼 Final Slide: Career Connection
- Highlight the importance of research, demo, and presentation
- Convey confidence, energy, and passion
- End with impact: technical skills open doors, presentation skills earn promotions
