## Core Success Principles

**Be "CRACKED" - The 3 Essential Skills:**
1. **SPEED** - Act quickly, submit on time, implement feedback fast
2. **FEEDBACK** - Be open to criticism and actively seek improvement
3. **ACTION** - Don't just listen - immediately implement what you learn

## Presentation Structure (2-3 minutes total)

### 1. TEAM SLIDE (10 seconds max)
- Brief personal intro
- Current status (school, job search, interview count)
- Keep it SHORT - this is just context, not the focus

### 2. PROBLEM SLIDE (10 seconds max)  
- Clearly define WHO your customer is
- Explain the specific challenge you're solving
- Show you understand the real-world impact
- Demonstrate research and thinking, not just coding

### 3. SOLUTION SLIDE (20 seconds)
- Explain your approach and methodology
- Highlight research you did independently
- Show different approaches you considered
- Demonstrate autonomy and proactive thinking

### 4. ARCHITECTURE DIAGRAM (included in 50-second total)
- Maximum 3 components/boxes
- Keep it simple and clean
- Avoid cluttering with too many logos/APIs
- Focus on core system flow

**Target: Team + Problems + Solution + Diagram = 50 seconds total**

### 5. LIVE DEMO (30-40 seconds)
- Show something that ACTUALLY WORKS
- Use production/deployed link if possible
- Focus on the end result and user experience
- This is the most important part - make it impressive

### 6. CODE WALKTHROUGH (30-40 seconds)
- Show the 2 HARDEST parts you solved
- Explain where you struggled and how you overcame it
- Demonstrate technical depth and problem-solving

### 7. FUTURE IMPROVEMENTS (10 seconds)
- What would you do with more time?
- Shows forward thinking and project vision

## Key Success Factors

**RESEARCH IS EVERYTHING**
- Ask tons of questions before you start coding
- Understand your audience, judges, and stakeholders
- Quality of results depends on quality of questions asked

**PRACTICE MAKES PERFECT**
- Practice your presentation 2-3 times minimum
- Time yourself - stick to the 2-3 minute limit
- Your coding ability won't matter if you can't present it well

**MAKE IT UNIQUE**
- Avoid generic demos that look like everyone else's
- Show personal research and independent thinking
- Demonstrate what YOU specifically contributed

**TECHNICAL DEPTH**
- Don't just show the pretty UI
- Prove you can solve hard technical problems
- Be specific about challenges and solutions

## Presentation Delivery Tips

**TIMING BREAKDOWN:**
- Team: 10 seconds
- Problem: 10 seconds  
- Solution: 20 seconds
- Diagram: 10 seconds
- Demo: 30-40 seconds
- Code: 30-40 seconds
- Future: 10 seconds
- **Total: 2-3 minutes**

**SPEAKING TIPS:**
- Speak with confidence and energy
- Focus on impact and results
- Show passion for the problem you're solving
- Practice until timing is natural
