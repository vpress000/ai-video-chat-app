## **Design Guidelines**

### **Color Scheme:**
- **Primary:** Deep blue (#1e3a8a) - trust, technology
- **Secondary:** Bright orange (#f97316) - energy, innovation  
- **Accent:** Clean white (#ffffff) - clarity, simplicity
- **Text:** Dark gray (#374151) - readability

### **Typography:**
- **Headlines:** Bold, sans-serif (Arial or Helvetica)
- **Body Text:** Clean, readable (Calibri or similar)
- **Size:** Large enough for back row visibility

### **Visual Style:**
- **Minimal text per slide** (max 7 words per bullet)
- **High-contrast colors** for accessibility
- **Consistent icon style** (outline or filled)
- **Professional but approachable** imagery

### **Animation Suggestions:**
- **Slide transitions:** Simple fade or slide
- **Text animation:** Appear on click for pacing
- **No distracting effects** - content is king

---

## **Presenter Tips**

### **Timing Control:**
- **Practice with stopwatch** - aim for 2:45 to allow for natural pauses
- **Use slide transitions** to pace yourself
- **Have backup slides** ready if running short

### **Engagement Techniques:**
- **Start with audience interaction** (raised hands)
- **Use "you" language** throughout
- **Make eye contact** with different sections
- **Vary your voice pace** and volume

### **Technical Backup:**
- **PDF version** in case of PowerPoint issues
- **Presenter notes printed** as backup
- **Demo video file** if live demo fails
- **Phone hotspot** for internet backup

### **Q&A Preparation:**
- **"How does the AI work?"** → GPT-4 Turbo with video transcript context
- **"What videos does it support?"** → Any YouTube video with available audio
- **"Is it free?"** → Currently in development, pricing TBD
- **"Can it work offline?"** → Requires internet for AI processing
- **"What's next?"** → Multi-language support, mobile app, enterprise features
